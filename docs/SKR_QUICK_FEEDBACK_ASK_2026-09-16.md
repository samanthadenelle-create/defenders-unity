# SKR in Echoes of Elarion — quick feedback ask

We use SKR two ways, and never hold it:

1. **Pay with SKR** — packs in our in-game market are priced in SKR next to SOL. Live today.
2. **Reward SKR stakers** — we read the player's native SKR staking position (read-only, server
   verified) and give staking players extra in-game attempts and perks. Bigger and longer stake
   climbs a tier ladder. Live today for the first perk; more perks are built and waiting.

No vault, no custody, no SKR payouts, no SKR that buys army strength.

## Three questions

1. Is reading native SKR staking to unlock in-game perks the kind of use you want to see?
2. Should SKR pricing in the market be the default, or stay side by side with SOL?
3. Anything on this list you would not want us to do?
